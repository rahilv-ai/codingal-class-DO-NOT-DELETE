instructions 📋

ad or qd for moving left right
space, w or z to jump
n for slide & c for dash
j or mouse press to shoot bullets (free)
v for medkit which heals 30 health (10 coins)
f for shield which makes you immune to bullets for 5 seconds (30 coins)
k to use your sword mouse press to use sword which does 5 damage each hit (cost free)
k to disable your sword (you cannot shoot while using a sword)
l to use lightning on your enemy which does 30 damage (cost 20 coins)
m to use a poison gas ability which lasts 10 second and does 3 damage every second (cost 20 coins)

side tips 📋

poison gas also does damage to you but 2 damage every seond instead of 3
your abilities occur at your mouse pointer
